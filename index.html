Our Story

CLM Renovations Ltd. was founded with a simple mission: to transform houses into dream homes with uncompromising quality and personal attention to detail.

What started as a dedicated handyman service has grown into a trusted renovation company, specializing in custom bathrooms, kitchens, tiling, and comprehensive home improvements. We combine professional expertise with modern techniques and quality materials.

We treat every project we undertake with the utmost care and professionalism. We understand that renovations are more than just construction — they're about creating spaces where families build memories, where comfort meets style, and where quality stands the test of time.

CLM Construction Ltd. was created just after we completed our Home Builders License with the province of BC. We are now expanding to new construction projects such as custom homes, carriage houses and more.

Common Questions

The most FAQ

What construction services does CLM offer in the Comox Valley?

The CLM Group provides a full range of residential and commercial construction services in the Comox Valley, including custom homes, home additions, carriage houses, full and partial home renovations, custom bathrooms, tiling, kitchens and decks.

What type of design services do you offer?

We can design spaces to suit your needs and provide personalized 3D designs of your future space. Depending on the project, an architect can be involved.

How much does a custom home or renovation cost?

Every project is unique, so pricing depends on factors such as size, design, materials, site conditions and project complexity. Detailed estimates are provided after an initial consultation.

Do you use subcontractors, and are they insured?

Yes. CLM works with trusted, specialized trade partners. All sub-trades are fully insured, hold valid BC trade certifications and maintain active WorkSafeBC coverage.

Is CLM Construction licensed and insured?

Absolutely. CLM Group is fully licensed and insured, giving clients confidence that projects are completed safely and professionally.

How long does a construction project usually take?

Project timelines vary depending on the size and complexity of the work. Smaller renovations may take a few weeks, while custom homes and major additions can take several months.

Why choose CLM Group for your Comox Valley project?

We are committed to quality craftsmanship, honest communication and reliable project management. We provide detailed estimates and keep clients updated throughout construction.
